About

3D generalist, environment artist and indie developer.

I'm Liam, a 3D generalist based in Stockholm. I studied 3D at Visual Magic and did an environment art internship on Project Solar, modeling props and buildings around the game's trim sheet.

Environment art for games is my main focus: hard-surface modeling, modular assets, texturing, lighting and set dressing in Unreal Engine and Unity. I also enjoy the technical side, like procedural modeling, simulation and effects in Houdini, Blender and Niagara.

On my own time I design and publish mobile games, and make 3D animation for my YouTube channel.
