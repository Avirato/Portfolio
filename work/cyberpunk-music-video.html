Stylized Cyberpunk Music Video

A three-week group project where we made a stylized music video inspired by cyberpunk visuals and atmosphere.

I modeled and textured assets, lit several scenes and set-dressed the apartment, rooftop and bus station. I also contributed to material creation, a small amount of animation (a liquid time-lapse) and simulated the broken bottle.

Role
Modeling, texturing, lighting, set dressing
Tools
Blender, Substance 3D Painter
