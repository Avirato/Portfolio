School assignment · 2024

Houdini Simulation

The task was to create a piece in Houdini that uses at least two different simulations.

I used a Vellum cloth simulation for the bouncy balls, and a particle simulation for the magical trail that guides them through the streets of San Francisco. The background is a photo sourced online, composited with the render in Nuke.

Tools
Houdini, Nuke
Focus
Vellum, particles, compositing
