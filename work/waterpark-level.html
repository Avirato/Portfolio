Final exam project · 2025

Indoor Waterpark

A level for my six-week final exam project: an eerie, semi-realistic indoor waterpark inspired by Eriksdalsbadet in Stockholm.

It's a first-person exploration game where the player searches the environment for a hidden code that unlocks the exit. My focus was environment design, procedural modeling and simulations.

Role
Environment art & level design
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.5
Tools
