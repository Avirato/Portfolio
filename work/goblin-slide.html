Goblin Slide

An endless downhill sled runner for Android, made and published on my own in Unity. I did the art, the VFX, the UI and the game itself.

Everything is built from low-poly assets with simple materials to keep it light on phones. This is a breakdown of how the art, effects and UI came together.
