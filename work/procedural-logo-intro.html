Houdini course · 2025

Procedural Logo Intro

My final project for a Houdini course: a movie-studio-style intro where objects spill out of a cup and seamlessly transition into the Visual Magic logo.

The setup is fully procedural, so both the objects and the logo can easily be swapped. The project explores procedural modeling, RBD destruction, Vellum grain simulation and a USD workflow.
